課題D：罠66種（SVG のもの全部。淫糸の巣・囁きの塔は PNG なので対象外）。Grok の画像生成（「品質」モード・正方形）に、===== で区切った1ブロックずつ、STYLE の行ごとそのまま貼る。
選んだ1枚を ~/grok_raw/D/<id>_<番号>.png に保存し、
  python3 newgame/tools/grok_fit.py sprite ~/grok_raw/D/<id>_<番号>.png newgame/assets/traps/<id>.png --pixel 2
で整え、data.js の G.TRAPS の該当の罠に art: "<id>.png" を足す（type の後ろ。ほかの値は触らない）。元の <id>.svg は消さずに残す。

STYLE は2種類。ブロックの頭に書いてあるものを使う。
  [物] … 床に置かれた物・台・装置
  [面] … 壁や床の一面（四角い板状に見せる）

[物] STYLE: a single dungeon trap object sprite for a Japanese 2D dungeon RPG, in the style of Super Famicom era JRPG map chips and Japanese RPG Maker map objects. Tiny low-resolution pixel art, about 48 pixels tall and enlarged so the big square pixels are clearly visible. One object only, seen from the front and slightly from above, its base at the bottom center of the image. Flat colors with a single shade tone, about 10 colors, dark outline, simple readable silhouette, almost no small details. Dusky dungeon colors with pink and violet accents. Whole object inside the frame, no characters, no people, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark, no green anywhere on the object, flat solid pure green (#00FF00) background. Not western cartoon, not a detailed illustration.

[面] STYLE: a single square dungeon wall or floor panel sprite for a Japanese 2D dungeon RPG, in the style of Super Famicom era JRPG map chips and Japanese RPG Maker map tiles. Tiny low-resolution pixel art, a square panel about 48 pixels wide and enlarged so the big square pixels are clearly visible, seen straight from the front. Flat colors with a single shade tone, about 10 colors, dark outline around the panel, simple readable pattern. Dusky dungeon colors with pink and violet accents. The panel fills most of the image, centered, no characters, no people, no shadow, no text, no watermark, no green anywhere on the panel, flat solid pure green (#00FF00) background around it.

共通の NG：人物を描かない（罠の上に人がいない）・血・卵や卵の塊に見える物・実在の虫・幽霊・骸骨・実在の宗教の印・文字。
